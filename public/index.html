Hello , I’m L. Boukela

I am a computer scientist developing AI-based solutions to enhance system reliability and security. My work spans cybersecurity and particle accelerators, addressing the evolving nature of computer network data in intrusion detection systems and the resource constraints involved in deploying ML models at the European XFEL. I combine the academic rigor of a scientist with the execution focus of an engineer, my work has led to impactful solutions and publications, cross-disciplinary collaborations, and research experience at international laboratories.
