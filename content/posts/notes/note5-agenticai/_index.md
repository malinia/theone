# Briefly

Agentic AI refers to AI systems that can **reason, plan, make decisions, and take actions** to achieve a goal. Unlike traditional AI systems that mainly respond to a single prompt, AI agents can work through multiple steps and adapt their actions based on the results.

A typical agent combines a **large language model with tools, memory, and a planning or reasoning process**. For example, an agent might search the web, retrieve information from a database, analyze the results, and then use a software tool to complete a task.

One of the key ideas behind Agentic AI is the **ability to interact with its environment**. Instead of simply generating an answer, the system can decide what action should be taken next and evaluate whether that action produced the desired result.

Agentic AI can be particularly useful for **automation, research, data analysis, software development, and complex workflows** where tasks require multiple decisions rather than a single response.

What I find interesting about Agentic AI is the shift from *"AI that answers questions"* to *"AI that can accomplish tasks."* The challenge is not only making agents capable, but also making them **reliable, controllable, and transparent**.
